# Alex Klick



## Opening

When I built a RAG ingestion pipeline to process clinical and regulatory documents for a Fortune-200 biopharma client, I learned how much precision matters when the stakes are high. Sub-section level PDF chunking, complex RTF table extraction, and rigorous quality evaluation weren't abstract engineering problems—they were the difference between trustworthy outputs and costly errors in regulated submissions. That experience shaped my conviction that AI in healthcare-adjacent domains demands a different standard of care than typical LLM applications. Cohere Health's mission to automate prior authorization through intelligent clinical decision support represents exactly the kind of high-consequence, high-reward problem I want to work on.

## Role Alignment

My background building document intelligence and retrieval systems in regulated biopharma environments transfers directly to the challenges Cohere Health solves. I've spent the past three years at Credera designing RAG architectures, deploying vector search systems, and building modular agent components—all of which map closely to the technical demands of a clinical intelligence platform processing 12M+ prior authorizations annually. While my experience has been concentrated on the backend and AI/LLM layer rather than full-stack web development, I've consistently partnered with design and business analysts to ship user-facing features, and I'm eager to expand into more comprehensive full-stack work. Similarly, while I haven't worked directly with HIPAA compliance, the rigor required for biopharma regulatory submissions has given me a grounded appreciation for compliance-driven development and the discipline it demands.

## Evidence Highlights

At Credera, I architected a RAG ingestion pipeline that enabled precise sub-section chunking of complex clinical PDFs for a Fortune-200 biopharma client, directly supporting regulatory submission workflows. I built the accompanying vector database and reranker system, leading a quality evaluation process to select the best sentence-transformers model for production deployment at scale. More recently, I designed modular agent components using structured JSON outputs, piloting with approximately 100 users before expanding to 300+ with broader enterprise rollout planned. Throughout this work, I've mentored junior engineers on performance and reliability practices, and maintained a steady partnership with cross-functional stakeholders to convert ambiguous requirements into shipped, observable systems.
