More recently, I've been shipping modular agent components that started with ~100 pilot users and are now expanding toward 300+ with enterprise rollout planned. Throughout, I've mentored junior engineers on agentic workflow reliability and partnered closely with design and business analysts to close the loop between user feedback and shipped features.
