I've mentored engineers through these problems and built systems that handle them. Your focus on enterprise-grade models with multilingual capabilities and deployable infrastructure resonates because I've been the engineer trying to make generic models work in high-consequence domains like healthcare. I understand viscerally why retrieval quality, factual grounding, and controllable outputs matter when accuracy affects real decisions.

I'd bring production-hardened instincts about what breaks at scale and genuine enthusiasm for working closer to the model layer. I'd welcome the chance to discuss how my experience building agentic systems at the application layer could contribute to Cohere's mission.
