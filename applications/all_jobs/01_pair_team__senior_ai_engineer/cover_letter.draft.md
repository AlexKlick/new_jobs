# Alex Klick


Earlier this year, I watched a pilot group of ~100 users interact with an agent orchestration system I had architected—navigating structured workflows, populating JSON schemas dynamically, and providing feedback that shaped our next sprint. That moment crystallized why I build AI systems: not for the technology itself, but for the tangible utility it delivers in complex, consequential domains. Pair Team's mission to serve Medicaid and Medicare populations through AI-enabled care coordination speaks directly to that motivation. The Senior AI Engineer role represents an opportunity to bring production multi-agent experience to healthcare workflows where retrieval quality, trust, and reliability are not nice-to-haves—they are essential.


My background aligns closely with what Pair Team is building. Over the past four years at Credera, I have shipped RAG systems for Fortune-200 biopharma clients, designed modular agent components using the Agents SDK, and built vector retrieval infrastructure that scaled from prototype to enterprise deployment. I am comfortable moving from ambiguous requirements to observable production systems, and I have done so in regulated environments where document accuracy directly impacts downstream decisions. The healthcare compliance-checking work I delivered for Janssen gave me exposure to the constraints and stakes of medical-adjacent systems—experience that few applicants in the general AI engineering pool can claim. While I have not yet used LangGraph or Langfuse/LangSmith in production, I have deep experience with LangChain orchestration patterns, the Claude Agents SDK, and building evaluation frameworks for retrieval quality. I am confident I can translate that foundation to your stack quickly.


Two projects illustrate the kind of work I would bring to Pair Team. First, I designed a RAG ingestion pipeline for a biopharma regulatory team that required sub-section-level PDF chunking with precise boundary detection. The system processed thousands of documents and enabled faster, more accurate regulatory submissions. Second, I built a vector database and reranker architecture for Credera's internal enterprise chat tool, leading a comparative evaluation of sentence-transformers models and deploying the winning approach at scale. Both projects required connecting retrieval architecture decisions directly to user outcomes—chunking granularity, embedding quality, and reranking precision all affected what users actually saw and trusted. That feedback loop between technical choices and real-world utility is where I do my best work.


Pair Team's position as a public benefit corporation serving vulnerable populations through technology is rare in this industry. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my production experience with multi-agent systems, document intelligence, and regulated-domain AI delivery could contribute to your mission. Thank you for your consideration.
