What draws me to M3 USA specifically is the opportunity to apply these retrieval and agent capabilities in service of healthcare professionals who depend on accurate, trustworthy information. My experience working with biopharma regulatory teams gave me a practical understanding of compliance-conscious development and the stakes involved when AI outputs inform clinical or business decisions. I'm comfortable owning the full lifecycle of AI features, from prototyping through production hardening, and I've regularly partnered with design and business stakeholders to translate feedback into roadmap changes.

I should be direct about where I'm still growing: my hands-on experience with REST API design and cloud infrastructure is less developed than my work with LLM and retrieval systems. That said, my background in building production backends with FastAPI and Pydantic has given me a foundation I'm eager to extend, and I learn quickly when shipping complete features is the goal.
