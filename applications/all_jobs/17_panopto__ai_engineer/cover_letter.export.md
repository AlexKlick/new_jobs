The technical challenges Panopto faces—indexing video content, building tutoring interactions, designing evaluation frameworks—are problems I've already solved in document form.

But the retrieval fundamentals translate: chunking strategies, embedding quality, reranking precision, and evaluation frameworks are modality-agnostic. My experience building AI systems where factual correctness is non-negotiable has given me a working understanding of the constraints that compliance requirements impose. I'm genuinely interested in learning the video domain and contributing to a remote-first team building something useful at scale.
