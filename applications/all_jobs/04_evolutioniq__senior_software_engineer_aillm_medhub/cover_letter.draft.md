EvolutionIQ's Medhub platform—which synthesizes complex medical documents for insurance claims—resonates with that same mission: using AI to improve decision quality where it genuinely matters. The opportunity to contribute to an AI guidance platform that helps people navigate difficult medical and insurance situations is exactly the kind of work that drew me to this field.

## Role Alignment

My experience over the past three years at Credera has centered on exactly the challenges Medhub faces: building retrieval systems that surface the right information from complex documents, deploying LLM-based features in production environments, and ensuring outputs are trustworthy enough for consequential decisions. I've designed sub-section PDF chunking pipelines, built vector databases and reranker systems serving tens of thousands of documents, and led model-quality evaluations to select sentence-transformer approaches that scaled to production. While my background emphasizes retrieval, embeddings-based systems, and architecture over fine-tuning specifically, I've worked extensively with OpenAI APIs, LangChain, and prompt engineering—and I'm genuinely eager to deepen my fine-tuning experience in a domain where retrieval quality and factual correctness are non-negotiable.

## Evidence Highlights

At Credera, I built and deployed a RAG system for an enterprise chat tool now used daily by hundreds of consultants, designing the vector database architecture and leading the evaluation process that selected our production reranker model. More recently, I've been shipping modular agent components for enterprise workflows, piloting with ~100 users and expanding toward 300+ as we plan broader rollout. Across these projects, I've mentored junior engineers on agentic system reliability and partnered closely with design and business analysts to ensure what we build actually serves user needs.
